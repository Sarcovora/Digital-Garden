Three kinds of notes: *fleeting*, *literature* and *permanent*.

## Fleeting
Quick captures that live in `04 - Fleeting` until they are processed, see [[Reading Lists]].

## Literature
What a source says, in your own words, with a reference.

## Permanent
One idea per note, written so that future you can use it. Link to neighbours with [[Home|wikilinks]].
