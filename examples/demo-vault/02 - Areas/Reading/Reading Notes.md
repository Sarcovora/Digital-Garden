Notes on reading well. Start with [[How to Read a Book]], then move on to [[Note-taking]].

## Why bother
Reading is not a race.
A single line break is kept as a line break,
just like it is in Obsidian.

> [!tip] Rule of thumb
> If a paragraph does not change what you do, it does not need a note.
