## Four levels
1. **Elementary**: decode the words.
2. **Inspectional**: skim to learn what the book is about.
3. **Analytical**: chew the book, ask what it claims and why.
4. **Syntopical**: read several books on one theme and compare.

## Questions to ask
- What is the book about *as a whole*?
- What is being said, and how?
- Is it true, in whole or in part?
- What of it?

> [!quote] On marginalia
> Writing in a book is the highest form of respect you can pay it.
