## Basics
- Same wake time every day, **even on weekends**
- No screens for the last 30 minutes
- Room cool, dark and quiet

> [!warning] Caffeine
> Half-life is about five to six hours. A 3 pm coffee is still half there at 9 pm.

> [!info]- Why a consistent wake time?
> It anchors the body clock far more than bedtime does.
