## Ingredients
- [x] 500 g bread flour
- [x] 350 g water
- [ ] 100 g active starter
- [ ] 10 g salt

## Method
1. Mix flour and water, rest 1 hour.
2. Add starter and salt, fold.
3. Bulk ferment 4 to 6 hours with a fold every 30 minutes.
4. Shape, then cold proof overnight.
5. Bake at 245 °C in a covered pot: 20 minutes lid on, 20 minutes lid off.

> [!success] Done when
> The crust sings when it cools.

==Do not slice for an hour.== Seriously.
